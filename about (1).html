About Me

Kali McEuen

I am a Business Management student in the Marriott School of Business at Brigham Young University and am entering my final year. I grew up in Arizona where I met my husband, Brayden, while we were both running track in high school. Today, we are both hurdlers competing for BYU.

My long-term goal is to combine my passion for business, creativity, and event planning by owning a wedding venue and becoming a wedding planner. Athletics has taught me leadership, discipline, perseverance, and teamwork that I will carry into my future career.
